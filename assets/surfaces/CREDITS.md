# Surface textures: credits and licences

Every texture in this folder is a photographic PBR material from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), by Lennart Demes and contributors, released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain; no attribution required, given here anyway). Each licence was read on the asset's own page (the "CC0 license, making them free to use without attribution - even in commercial circumstances" line) on 2026-10-19. Licence text: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>, ambientCG's terms: <https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/>.

Each folder holds `albedo.jpg` (the Color map), `normal.jpg` (NormalGL, the OpenGL convention Godot reads) and `roughness.jpg`, from the 1K-JPG download, re-encoded at JPEG quality 85. A source that is 1024x512 (brick, plaster, concrete_clean, wood_planks, corrugated) was stacked twice vertically into 1024x1024, which keeps it seamless and lets one uniform world scale cover every kind; the displacement, ambient occlusion and DirectX normal maps are not shipped.
